## Post completo

Italy kept its positive outlook last night. For Italian borrowers, it was not the most important number of the day.

The facts: S&P confirmed Italy at BBB+ with a positive outlook. The BTP–Bund spread closed at 104 bp, the Italian 10-year at 4.41%. In the US, the 10-year Treasury stayed above 5%, and futures price a Fed hike on 9 December at 100% (CME FedWatch via CNBC). Fed Vice Chair Jefferson said inflation is now "the more important signal", and that AI could push the neutral rate higher.

Three scenarios for anyone refinancing in 2027:

1. Higher for longer. The neutral rate moves up, as Jefferson suggests. Base rates stay high, and leverage sustainable today becomes the ceiling, not the floor.

2. Growth scare. AI equity correction (the Fed's top-cited risk) pushes yields down but widens credit spreads. Cheaper base rate, more expensive margin, fewer lenders.

3. Soft landing. Inflation eases with energy, the spread compresses after a 2027 upgrade. The best case, and the one most plans already assume.

My view: a plan that works only in scenario 3 is a hope, not a plan. Stress the margin, not just the base rate.
