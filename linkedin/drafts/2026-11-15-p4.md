# When investment-grade credit pays 6%, your buyout model has a new competitor.

- **Argomento:** 6% credit yields as the new hurdle
- **Perché interessa al pubblico:** PE, private credit, LP e corporate development.
- **Messaggio principale:** Il rendimento del credito di qualità è diventato il costo opportunità esplicito dell'equity privato.
- **Tesi:** Con rendimenti del credito al 6% e Treasury sopra il 5%, il premio richiesto all'equity dei buyout cresce mentre il costo del debito sale: i multipli d'ingresso devono scendere o la leva deve calare.

## Post completo

When investment-grade credit pays 6%, your buyout model has a new competitor.

Bloomberg's Credit Weekly on Saturday: "Juicy 6% Yields Gain Appeal After Nvidia Drama." Same week, the 10-year Treasury traded above 5% and markets priced more Fed hikes. Bitcoin hit a three-month low on Sunday.

The trend: after a volatile week in AI equities, money is moving towards fixed income that pays a real yield.

For private equity, this matters in two ways at once.

First, the cost of debt rises. Same leverage, higher interest, less free cash flow to pay down debt.

Second, the opportunity cost rises. An LP can get 6% from quality credit with daily liquidity. A buyout with a 10-year lock-up has to clear that with a wide margin.

Put the two together and the arithmetic changes. If exit multiples stay where they are, entry multiples need to come down or leverage needs to come down. Usually both.

[Spazio per la tua esperienza: es. come cambia l'IRR equity in un modello LBO quando il costo del debito sale di 100 punti base, senza dettagli riservati.]

This is not a call that credit beats equity. It is a reminder that the hurdle rate in a deal model is not a constant. It moves with the yield on the alternative.

What equity return would you now need to justify a buyout over a 6% credit allocation?
