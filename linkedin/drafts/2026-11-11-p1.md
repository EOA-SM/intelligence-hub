# A 50bp Fed hike would not change my base case. It would change my stress case.

- **Argomento:** Rate shock and refinancing models
- **Perché interessa al pubblico:** CFO, lender, leveraged finance e PE: chiunque abbia un rifinanziamento nei prossimi 18 mesi.
- **Messaggio principale:** Il rischio per le capital structure non è il livello dei tassi ma la velocità con cui cambiano gli scenari.
- **Tesi:** Molti modelli usano stress su ricavi e CapEx ma trattano il tasso come input fisso. Con la curva che si muove di decine di punti in una settimana, il tasso va stressato come una variabile operativa.
- **Domanda finale:** When you stress-test a refinancing, do you shock the rate curve, or do you take the forward curve as given?
- **Fonte:** Reuters, 11/11/2026 — https://www.933thedrive.com/2026/11/10/bonds-reel-as-hot-inflation-fuels-jumbo-fed-hike-fears/

## Post completo

A 50bp Fed hike would not change my base case. It would change my stress case.

Overnight, bonds sold off globally after a hotter US inflation print, and markets started pricing the risk of a half-point hike at the Fed's 8–9 December meeting. Japan's 10-year yield rose 7bp to 2.755%. Equities in Asia barely moved.

That split matters for anyone who builds capital-structure models.

Most refinancing models I see stress revenue and CapEx hard (ARPU down, build costs up, delays) but treat the rate as an input: the forward curve on the day the model was built. That worked when curves moved slowly. It doesn't work when the curve reprices by tens of basis points in a week.

Three things I'd put in every leveraged model right now:

1. A rate shock as its own scenario, not buried inside the downside case. +100bp on the floating portion, held for the life of the facility.
2. The hedge expiry date next to the debt maturity. The real cliff is often the swap rolling off, not the loan.
3. Covenant headroom under the combined case: rate shock plus a mild operating miss. That's usually where the problem shows up.

[Spazio per la tua esperienza: es. come avete costruito gli stress test nel rifinanziamento da 120 milioni, senza dettagli riservati.]

None of this predicts what the Fed will do. It just stops a board from approving a structure that only works if the curve stays where it was on the day of the model.

When you stress-test a refinancing, do you shock the rate curve, or do you take the forward curve as given?
